# GRIMGE Web Prototype Status

## Implemented

- `RUNE_SPELL_MASTERY_CANON.md` records the current authoritative Rune/Mastery design: 17 Standard Base Runes, 14 Mastery Trees plus Arcane Mastery, standalone Base-Rune value, selective two/three-rune grammar, Concept + Concept validity, invalid-combination fallback, reusable effect/world-object architecture, Special Rune rules, and horizontal-progression constraints. Current prototype recipes are explicitly a legacy vertical slice pending separately approved canonical recipe data.
- `TRANSFORMATION_SYSTEM_CANON.md` records the authoritative long-term three-source transformation target: Class Tree → Class form, Primal Bond → Manifestation/Hybrid/Fusion, and 14 Masteries → Mastery forms. It explicitly keeps existing Focus Ascendant, Eidolon Mantle and Ninefold Beast Form as prototype vertical-slice effects rather than implicitly finalizing the future transformation architecture.

- Browser Canvas prototype with fixed 1024×576 simulation and DPR-aware backing buffers.
- PWA/iOS shell: `manifest.webmanifest`, Apple standalone meta tags, `viewport-fit=cover`, GRIMGE Home-Screen naming, and branded derived app icons are all relative to `public/`, so the same deployment works at a GitHub Pages domain root or repository subpath. iOS safe insets are converted from physical CSS pixels into logical HUD coordinates; gameplay canvas remains edge-to-edge while critical HUD controls move clear of cut-outs/Home Indicator. Installed iOS standalone launches also reserve a small Home-Indicator clearance if Safari reports its inset late.
- Active matches are landscape-only at runtime. Portrait replaces the tiny scaled game with a full-screen GRIMGE “rotate device” screen, clears held input, pauses match simulation/timers, and resumes the same match on returning to landscape. The outer stage is edge-to-edge: the 1024×576 combat field keeps its constant logical height while a variable-width visual camera adds horizontal decorative arena overscan. This removes 16:9 side pillars without stretching sprites or altering combat geometry.
- Heroes render at an explicit **120% visual scale** (`74px × 1.20 = 88.8px`) for stronger character/cosmetic readability. Player and enemy Wizard share it; minion/summon sizes remain authored separately, and hero colliders stay `24×54` so movement, feet anchoring, hit ranges, and tuned physics remain unchanged.
- 2.5D state is `x`, continuous `z`, `surfaceHeight`, and independent `elevation`; render `y` is projected only.
- Main arena depth is z=.06–.94. Minion preferences use broad z=.20/.52/.84 lanes; the player remains unrestricted across the ground plane.
- Castle gameplay is split intentionally: lower Castle gate/objective cores at ground height, plus upper battlement surfaces for Wizard spawn/combat.
  - Blue upper battlement: x=94–204, z=.30–.68, base height 145; access x=204–292.
  - Red upper battlement: x=820–930, z=.30–.68, base height 145; access x=732–820.
  - Spawns: blue `(146,.48)`, red `(878,.48)`.
  - Leaving an upper surface preserves height and falls rather than snapping.
- Objective flow is authoritative: Tower → Castle → final Wizard. Castles are protected until their Tower falls, have permanent HP, and disable their team's future respawns once destroyed.
- Towers target only hostile mobile combatants (Wizards and minions), never Towers or Castles. A destroyed Tower neither targets nor fires.
- Tower/Castle HP is shown above a damaged objective. Player melee and Fireball both use the ordinary combat/spell path against Towers; Fireball's structural explosion radius now matches its valid objective contact volume.
- Minions retain target locks, light separation, lane preferences, and objective progression without player lane locking. When pushing a structure they converge tightly enough for both melee and ranged attacks to connect, then continue to the unlocked Castle.
- Match lifecycle is separate from objective phase: `Menu → Running → Ending (0.9s) → Results`; `Rematch` reconstructs match state without reload; `Return to Hub` reaches the minimal menu.
- `PLAY` and `REMATCH` use immediate pointer input with an accessible click fallback. A duplicate browser click cannot reset an already running match.
- Results display victory/defeat, final match time, objective state, Wizard kills, Rematch, and Hub controls. Enter rematches; Escape returns to Hub.
- Rune drawing is constrained to the current three-card `runeHand`. Tapping the mobile arcane circle opens Arcane Focus; a second tap locks in immediately, or a completed stroke auto-locks after two seconds. Non-hand or unrecognised gestures leave the mode active but clear the old drawing through a DPR-safe physical-canvas clear; Escape cancels.
- Rune deck foundation: exactly three physical rune cards are drawn into `runeHand`. A correctly drawn hand card moves immediately to the deck back and a replacement is drawn. Recognition is restricted to those current hand cards.
- Rune recognition is deliberately forgiving rather than binary. Valid gestures are graded `C / B / A / S` (S maximum): C is a usable but weaker cast, while A/S increase spell damage, ground coverage, duration/status time, knockback where relevant, and particle density. The grade stays on the orbiting slot; a Grimoire fusion averages the participating rune quality.
- Correctly drawn runes create separate `slottedSpells` rather than an unlimited prepared list. The selected slot glows in front of the Wizard while the remaining slots trail behind. `SWAP` rotates the selected slot and `CAST` fires only that slot. Grimoire first fuses compatible orbiting runes into one ready combination; pressing `CAST` then fires it. With no valid combination, Grimoire fires one random slot and clears the others. The Grimoire explanation belongs to the player portrait rather than the Grimoire action.
- Center rune cards, Grimoire, and Arcane Circle are now code-rendered controls instead of card/book/circle screenshot assets. CSS blocks iOS/WebKit image callouts and drag behavior on UI controls.
- Mobile viewport is locked to the game surface (`maximum-scale=1`, `user-scalable=no`, `touch-action:none`); double-click, gesture-start, drag, and context-menu gestures are prevented by the game input layer.
- The runtime viewport is an edge-to-edge `100vw × 100dvh` surface. `updateViewport()` keeps the authored 576 logical height, derives `renderWidth = max(1024, 576 × viewportAspect)`, and centres the unchanged combat field with `cameraOffsetX`. The screen-space HUD uses that dynamic logical width but scales from actual canvas height, so cards, heroes, and action controls retain their perceived size while wide devices gain visible horizontal world space.
- Mobile attack, jump, dash, and cast use `pointerdown` rather than delayed click input. A top-right fullscreen button requests browser fullscreen and landscape orientation where the browser permits it.
- Rune cards are the primary Draw controls: tap a hand card to open Arcane Focus for that exact card, then tap any rune card again to lock/close. In the alternate layout, double-tapping a free left-side area also opens Arcane Focus; double-tapping there again locks/closes it. Classic deliberately retains its central Draw circle in the right action cluster.
- The hub Settings control toggles a compact alternate touch layout. Classic keeps its original fixed lower-left joystick and right action cluster; alternate uses a dynamic left-thumb joystick, a central right Cast, Swap at 10 o'clock, Block at 7 o'clock, and a vertical right-side Jump control.
- Right-side touch combat gestures are deliberately mapped to the current compact combat foundation: tap = normal combo, swipe up = uppercut, swipe right = heavy forward strike, and airborne swipe down = dive attack.
- Aura Shock is the `AQUA + FULGUR` rune combination: 20 mana, 3.5s cooldown, short radial damage and true x/z knockback. It has no standalone hotkey or mobile button and does not damage objectives.
- Arcane Shield is available through `F` or the mobile `SHIELD` button: 30 mana, 8s cooldown, 90 absorption for up to 5 seconds; it breaks cleanly and is reset on respawn.
- The mobile action cluster uses a dominant circular `CAST`, with circular `BLOCK` and `SWAP` placed radially at roughly 7 and 10 o’clock. Contextual `MOUNT` appears only at 12 o’clock over Cast. `JUMP` is intentionally a vertical press/swipe rail with an upward arrow rather than a second circular action. Draw remains the explicit Classic rune circle or the existing card/left-double-tap interaction in Alternate. No standalone Shock/Dash/Attack action buttons remain there.
- The Grimoire, Skill Tree, Deck Builder, and Match Menu now use responsive full-screen backdrops: tapping outside their panels closes the overlay. The top-right `☰` Match Menu pauses the live match and provides Resume, Restart Match, touch-layout switching, Fullscreen, and Return to Hub without a page reload.
- The vertical Jump rail is offset to the right of the Cast clock-face cluster, preserving a clear horizontal gap while keeping its entire vertical range inside the action-group bounds.
- The boot/return Hub now uses the supplied **separate** start-menu assets as an actual responsive scene: the cosmic background, GRIMGE logo, Astral profile portrait, currency symbols and the individual Classes/Wardrobe/Grimoire/Class Tree/Primals/Mastery Tree/Transformations button artworks are composed as independent layers. The flattened concept image is not used as a live screen. Only true actions are interactive: Classes opens the playable hero selector, Primals opens companion selection, Grimoire opens the existing Deck page, Class Tree opens the existing Skill page, BATTLE opens Game Modes, and Settings opens real settings. Currency, logo and background remain display-only; unfinished Wardrobe/Mastery/Transformations/Messages/Friends routes give an honest prototype notice.
- `SHIELD` is a held physical front guard (`F` on keyboard): it visually appears in front of the Wizard, slows movement, spends stamina, and strongly reduces only frontal hits. It has an intentionally hidden 100-point stability meter: each blocked melee hit costs 10; at zero, the Wizard is guard-broken and stunned. A fresh 0.18-second guard is a Perfect Block that reflects minion/tower spell projectiles and Fireballs. It is separate from magic.
- `AQUA + TERRA` forms `ARCANE AEGIS`, the 30-mana temporary protective aura (90 absorption, 8s cooldown). `AQUA + FULGUR` remains the offensive Aura Shock combination.
- `TERRA + TERRA` forms `STONE WALL`. The deck contains a second Terra card so it can be drawn normally. Each Wizard may maintain one short-lived wall: it blocks hostile projectiles and hostile mobile movement while low actors are blocked, but can be jumped over. It is an active spell entity with normal cleanup, not a UI-only effect.
- Summoning vertical slice: `AQUA + TERRA + VENTUS` summons a fast Spirit Wolf; `FULGUR + TERRA + TERRA` summons a durable Siege Golem that prefers objectives; `IGNIS + TERRA + FULGUR` invokes a short, high-impact Dragon breath strike. Summons are real targetable mobile combat entities, use the normal enemy/objective target queries, attack, expire, and are cleared on rematch/results/hub. One summon of each role can be maintained per Wizard.
- Mount vertical slice: own Beast Familiars, Spirit Wolves, Storm Wolves, Siege Golems, and Rune Golems are mountable at close range. `R` toggles keyboard mounting; on touch, the circular `MOUNT` button is hidden unless a valid nearest mount candidate is in range, then appears exactly at 12 o’clock above `CAST`. Candidate selection has a small hysteresis margin to avoid flicker. Riding reuses the summon’s ground physics, ramp/fall transitions, and speed, pauses its autonomous attack AI, visibly renders the Wizard above it, and dismounts automatically if the creature expires or dies. Jump is a quick dismount; Dragon and Void Spider are deliberately not mounts.
- Spectral form vertical slice: `BESTIA + TERRA + VOID` forms the temporary `EIDOLON MANTLE` around the Wizard. It is an original procedurally rendered violet guardian silhouette, not a copied character asset. It lasts about 10 seconds (drawing grade scales duration), mitigates incoming damage/knockback, and empowers the existing melee chain. It costs 45 mana and has an 18-second cooldown; death and respawn clear it.
- Feral form vertical slice: `BESTIA + IGNIS + VOID` forms the original `NINEFOLD BEAST FORM`. It is an aggressive, mutually exclusive alternative to EIDOLON: roughly 8 seconds of faster movement, stronger melee, light damage resistance, and a procedural orange nine-tail rune aura. It costs 55 mana and has a 22-second cooldown.
- Upper Castle battlements are now Wizard-only spell-duel spaces. Minions always resolve to the main ground surface, spawn outside the access ramps, and will not acquire a Wizard standing on a battlement. Wizard melee is suppressed when both are on a battlement; enemy AI uses Fireball pressure there instead. Ranged minion bolts use an objective's authored depth footprint so all three lanes can legitimately damage a Tower without collapsing their formation early.
- Dark Lord is selectable as hero 6 / `DARK LORD`. A web-native fallback sprite based on the supplied dark-armour, crimson-crown reference is used if `public/assets/sprites/char_dark_lord.png` is absent. The supplied external reference PNG was not imported because the external asset-copy action was unavailable; its visual registration still requires manual validation once an approved copy is present.
- The top HUD now consumes the actual Wizard HP/MP values (rather than Tower HP); the player also has a live world-space health bar. The rune deck is independently anchored, reduced, and centred at the bottom of the logical HUD.
- Mage progression vertical slice: a locally persisted `MageProfile` gains XP from each successfully logged rune (`C/B/A/S = 6/10/14/18 XP`) and match completion (25 defeat / 60 victory XP). Every level grants one skill point. Fresh profiles begin with the five elemental cards; Mage levels 2/3/4 unlock `BESTIA`/`KONSTRUKT`/`VOID` as genuine deck-eligible collection cards. Rune cards and the Grimoire show per-rune level/XP; each rune level gives a modest quality-independent spell power/area/duration/particle bonus without replacing drawing grade.
- Hub Deckbuilder: `RUNE DECK` opens the persistent collection/loadout view. A match deck contains exactly 10 cards and has a two-copy limit per unlocked rune. The hand and all replacements are drawn only from this selected deck; an incomplete deck blocks PLAY with clear feedback. Fresh profiles receive two copies of each starter element. Newly unlocked Bestia/Konstrukt/Void cards are added to the collection and must be deliberately included in a later deck build.
- Summon expansion: `BESTIA + TERRA + VENTUS` summons the **World Rune Serpent**. It is a mountable ground-based lane-control creature using the supplied `summon_rune_snake.png` asset. Its normal summon attack launches nearby hostile units in elevation and slightly through depth; it uses the same surface/ramp/fall rules as other ground summons rather than hovering off a battlement.
- Summon readability/build pass: the bottom deck HUD displays every active allied summon with remaining seconds, HP percentage and `RIDING` state. The Summoning tree's Spirit Bond now directly grants both summon health and mount speed; Empowered Summons improves damage, Dragon Heart improves Dragon coverage, and Focused Casting extends sustained summons through the shared cast-quality duration path.
- The Hub now exposes a usable Mage Skill Tree with 12 data-driven nodes in four real branches: Elemental, Defense, Summoning, and Rune Mastery. Prerequisites and skill points are authoritative rather than UI-only. Current modifiers directly affect spell power/coverage/duration, combined-spell power, incoming damage, wall duration/coverage, Aura Shock radius/push, summon health/damage, Dragon coverage, and the Quick Draw C→B cast-quality floor.
- Combat-pressure pass: the Enemy Wizard now has a small authoritative defensive AI rather than a simple chase loop. It identifies pressure around its Tower/Castle, intercepts the player, raises a real Stone Wall against an objective push, casts Frost Nova at close range, fires at range, retreats toward its side when critically hurt, and spends/regenerates mana. At low health it uses the same Arcane Aegis shield path as the player.
- Focus vertical slice: successful rune logging, casts, and melee hits build a visible 0–100 Focus meter. Full Focus enables the compact bottom-deck `FOCUS` activation (or `T`) for an 8-second Focus Ascendant: movement, melee impact, and damage resistance improve, with a procedural violet aura. It resets cleanly on respawn and does not replace the rune-driven Eidolon/Ninefold forms.
- Build readability pass: the Hub now labels the currently dominant Mage specialization. Existing Elemental, Defense, Summoning, and Rune Mastery modifiers remain live gameplay modifiers; Focused Casting additionally grants +15% Focus gain, making the Rune Master path mechanically distinct.
- Melee baseline pass: the pre-weapon-system combat contract is now an explicit `MELEE_BASELINE` data table. Normal three-step attacks, uppercut, heavy lunge, and aerial dive retain the current arcade values; a short late-recovery normal-attack buffer makes the basic combo responsive without adding weapon-specific branches, Block, or Parry.
- Game-mode vertical slices now share one data-driven `GAME_MODES` contract and the existing combat/runtime path rather than being decorative Hub cards:
  - **Castle Siege** remains the complete Tower → Castle → final Wizard loop with two-sided lane waves.
  - **Invasion** is a one-sided, six-wave open-world defense run: the player holds the **left-side keep** and brings the persistent Grimoire; hostile waves enter from the open right side; there is no enemy Castle or lane Tower. Two blue defense Towers sit near the keep at back/front depth. Their destruction makes the keep materially harder to defend, but does not end the run by itself. A final Rift Tyrant ends the run.
  - **Dungeon** is the only level-zero run mode: the player spawns with no inherited Rune card, must first defeat a single Scout through normal melee, and only then receives the first choice of two Runes. It clears three visually advancing rooms, can rescue a companion in the route (the ally follows/fights until defeated), then ends at the Void Warden (Dark Lord Champion) boss. Its route parallax tracks forward movement and advances between rooms instead of using Castle/tower geometry.
- **Arena** is a Castle-free, no-respawn 2.5D melee duel with no Towers, Castles, or Minions. It starts with no deck and places collectible Rune relics around the map; a random contested Transformation Relic periodically grants one existing short transformation (Focus Ascendant, Eidolon Mantle, or Ninefold Beast) rather than a permanent account form.
  - Arena now has three **visible, jump-landed intermediate platforms** (left / raised centre / right). They are real 2.5D surfaces: fighters cannot walk up them as invisible steps, can land while descending, and fall correctly when leaving an edge.
  - Invasion and Dungeon explicitly use only the main ground surface; they no longer inherit hidden Castle battlements or a floating right-side plateau from the Siege map.
  - **Training** is an endless no-reward combat space; its opponent resets rather than ending the match.
  Only Dungeon uses the temporary level-zero Rune run. Siege, Invasion and Training retain the profile Grimoire. Arena is the deliberate melee-first exception: it starts empty and gains only map Rune relics. The existing responsive Free Play cards and Training action now launch these modes directly. Campaign, Ranked, Co-op, Custom Match, Tutorials, and their final progression/reward rules remain intentionally unimplemented.
